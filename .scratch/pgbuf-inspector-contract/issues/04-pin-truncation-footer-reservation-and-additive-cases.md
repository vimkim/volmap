# 04: Pin truncated scans, footer reservation and additive-evolution cases

**What to build:** A Volmap implementer can publish valid truncated scans as partial evidence with the honest reason, ignore additive fields and unknown in-scan frame kinds, and mark duplicate identities ambiguous, all against pinned bytes. The CUBRID serializer proves that a valid footer always fits and that its scan budget stops admitting pages before it would not.

**Blocked by:** 03 — Pin complete scans with the full semantic vocabulary.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] The contract specifies truncation: `truncated` is true only when traversal stopped before visiting every slot; `truncation_reason` is present only then and is the first of `slot-cap`, `page-cap`, `byte-cap`, `deadline` that applied; a cap reached exactly at complete traversal is not truncation; the next scan starts after the visited span; each slot is visited at most once per scan; no scan is merged with another; abnormal termination is a closed connection without a footer, and the consumer discards the unfinished assembly while retaining prior usable, unexpired evidence.
- [ ] The contract publishes the footer reservation bound as a constant equal to the largest canonical footer frame and requires the producer to reserve it before admitting each page frame.
- [ ] The serializer gains a scan budget that accounts emitted bytes and pages against the 64 MiB and 65,536-page caps with the footer bound reserved, refuses a page that would leave less than the bound or exceed the page cap, and always permits the footer afterward.
- [ ] Cases exist: one truncated scan per reason (four cases, `accepted-partial`); a hello, a header, a page and a footer each carrying additive unknown fields (accepted, ignored-field counts recorded); an unknown frame kind between header and footer (accepted, ignored, not counted in `page_count`); a complete scan with duplicate identities (accepted, duplicates listed as ambiguous, `page_count` counting raw frames). Truncated and duplicate streams are generated by the serializer; additive and unknown-frame streams are derived by documented, replayable mutation of canonical streams.
- [ ] Tests prove the footer bound equals the largest constructible footer, budget refusal at the boundary with footer admission after refusal, byte-exact reproduction of generated streams, reproduction of mutated streams from their documented mutation, and corpus integrity after regeneration with the revision incremented.
- [ ] Execution evidence with named cases and nonzero counts is recorded in this ticket's comments with the exact commit.
